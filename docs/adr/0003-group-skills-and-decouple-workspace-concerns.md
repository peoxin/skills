# Group Research Skills and Decouple Tooling Concerns

The skill source tree groups independently callable research leaves under `research/investigation/`, `research/experimentation/`, and `research/writing/`. Independent development tools live under the peer `tooling/` group. Groups are not skills or routers, and `npx skills` owns recursive discovery and installation. The experiment initializer creates only `data/`, `models/`, `benchmarks/`, `experiments/`, and `results/`, while Investigation and Writing preserve clear existing project locations or lazily default to `references/` and `writing/`; this supersedes ADR-0001 only for its `sources/` and unified-workspace decisions.
